Chapter 0 Instructor Key: Figma Decisions

Use this page while groups work. Do not print it with the student cards. The student page is Chapter 0 In-Class: Figma Decisions.

Desired Results

Students should leave able to say these five ideas in plain language:

  1. A frame is a phone screen with a size. A group is only a bundle of layers. Drawing on the empty canvas is not an app screen.
  2. Auto layout stacks and spaces items. Hand-nudging falls apart when one piece moves.
  3. A component is the master stamp. Instances stay linked. Copy-paste does not.
  4. A prototype is a tap path. Clicking in Design view does not play the flow. They need the blue arrow, then Present.
  5. Export the outer Welcome Screen frame. An inner text layer makes a cropped or blank PNG.

If a group still says “just copy the button” for every card, they have not split a component from a paste. Stay with Cards C and F until that clicks.

Answer Key

Correct move, why it works, and what to listen for
Card Correct move Why Wrong move Listen for
A Create an iPhone frame and put the layers inside A frame is the phone boundary. Title and button belong inside that screen, not on open canvas. Keep drawing on the blank canvas, or wrap it in a group. A group has no device size. Phone screen versus loose sketch
B Apply Auto layout Auto layout stacks like a VStack. Spacing stays even when one item moves. Keep nudging pixels. The stack breaks again on the next edit. Let Figma hold the stack
C Create a component, then use instances The master is the stamp. Instances update when the master changes. Copy-paste more buttons. Color changes stay only on the one they edited. Linked copies, not clones
D Duplicate the frame Option-drag copies the iPhone screen. Size and layout stay matched. Draw a tiny new rectangle. The second “screen” will not match the phone. Copy the whole phone, not a scrap
E Connect a prototype, then Present The blue arrow is the tap. Present plays it. Design view only edits. Keep clicking Get Started on the Design canvas. Nothing will play. Prototype tab, then the play icon
F Select the outer frame, then export Export follows the selected layer. The outer Welcome Screen frame is the full PNG. Export the selected text or auto layout group. The image is cropped or empty. Outer frame in the layers panel

Exit Tickets

They got it

  • A frame is a phone screen. A group is just a bundle of layers with no screen size.
  • I use a component so that when I change the button, every instance updates.

Not there yet

  • A frame and a group are the same.
  • Copy-paste is how you reuse a button.
  • Present is the same as looking at the Design canvas.

Debrief Script

Ask the whole class. Do not start a new Figma file. Do not open Xcode.

  1. After Card A, is a group a phone screen? No. Only a frame has a device size.
  2. After Card C, if they recolor the master, which copies change? Only instances, not pasted clones.
  3. After Card F, why was the PNG tiny? They exported an inner layer, not the outer frame.

If a Group Is Lost

Use one analogy only. Floor plan. The frame is the house walls. Auto layout is rooms that keep even spacing. A component is a stamp for the front door. An instance is every copy of that stamp. Prototype is walking from the porch to the next room. Export is a photo of the whole house, not one door handle.

If they finish early, have them rename one junk layer. Change Rectangle 12 or Frame 2 into a lab-style name such as Welcome Screen or PrimaryButton.
