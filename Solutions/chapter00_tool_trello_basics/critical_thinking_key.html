Chapter 0 Trello Agile Challenges: Instructor Key

Do not share this page with students. It is the answer key for Chapter 0 In-Class: Trello Agile Challenges.

Groups of four. About 20 to 25 minutes. Each group picks one challenge and builds one new Trello board. All four people must own a must-do card.

Desired results

  • Students treat the board as the conversation, not a second GroupMe.
  • They put one name on each must-do card. A card with no owner is a wish.
  • They keep tonight’s list small. Extra ideas go to a later list.
  • They can say, in one sentence, who owns which job without opening the group chat.

If-lost analogy

The Trello lab was a floor plan for one fake sprint. These challenges are still floor plans. The “house” is an RA inspection, a van leaving at 6 a.m., or a Friendsgiving. If a group is lost, ask: “Whose name is on dumpster / van / turkey?” Do not ask them to add more lists for fun.

Challenge 1: RA Inspection Friday

Elegant board

  1. Lists: Backlog (nice-to-clean) | Friday must | In Progress | Done.
  2. Four Friday-must cards only: Dumpster run (Jules), Kitchen (Kai), Living room vacuum (Morgan), Bathroom (Riley). Due Friday 10 a.m.
  3. Kai is not on Morgan’s vacuum card. Jules is not on Kai’s kitchen card.
  4. Done means a comment from that person: “bags at the chute, 9:40.” Hallway yelling is not an update.
RA Inspection Friday: listen for
Clumsy Why it fails What to praise
One card: “clean the apartment.” Jules and Kai on everything. Morgan and Riley extra. Four zones. Fail one, lose $200. A zone with no name does not get done. Four cards, four names, Friday due dates. One Done comment from the owner.

Challenge 2: Spring Break in One Van

Elegant board

  1. Lists: Later / maybe | Before Friday 6 a.m. | In Progress | Done.
  2. Four must-do cards: Book Airbnb (Casey), Confirm van (Alex), Gas money (Priya), Cooler (Devon). One owner each.
  3. WIP: four In Progress cards max. One per person. Playlist and extra suitcases sit in Later / maybe.
  4. Priya’s Gas money has a checklist: collect, Venmo, fill tank. Alex’s Confirm van is labeled Blocked with the comment “need a yes by Wed.”
Spring Break in One Van: listen for
Clumsy Why it fails What to praise
One mega-card: “spring break.” Twelve ideas in In Progress. Three people buying chips. Nobody on Airbnb. Starting everything is not a trip. Friday 6 a.m. does not wait. Airbnb and van are named. At most four In Progress. Blocked is honest. Gas money is a checklist, not a vibe.

Challenge 3: Friendsgiving Scope Creep

Elegant board

  1. Lists: Backlog | This Sunday | In Progress | Done.
  2. This Sunday stays four cards: Turkey (Avery), Sides (Sam), Drinks + ice (Jordan), Clean and chairs (Riley). Due Sunday.
  3. Sam still owns sides. The 1 a.m. fountain does not steal that card or Avery’s oven.
  4. Backlog: Chocolate fountain, Seating chart, no Sunday due date. Avery comments on the fountain: “After this dinner. $60 is the cap.”
Friendsgiving Scope Creep: listen for
Clumsy Why it fails What to praise
Avery and Sam argue in DMs. Jordan and Riley have no cards. Fountain is the only In Progress card. Dinner still needs turkey, sides, drinks, and a clean table. The fairy is extra, not the sprint. Four Sunday jobs, four names. Fountain parked in Backlog. Polite comment everyone can see.

Exit tickets

Good vs not-there-yet
Sentence Good Not there yet
We put one name on each card so that… …someone owns it and it actually happens / the person who missed GroupMe can still see who does dumpster. …Trello wanted a member / it looks official.
A new wish that is not tonight’s work should go… …to the backlog (or Later / maybe), with no due date for tonight, and a comment that parks it. …into In Progress / into the group chat / onto Jules.

Debrief script (about 4 minutes)

  1. Ask one inspection group: “If Jules is at their girlfriend’s, who takes the dumpster?” Push them to point at Jules’s card, not “we’ll all help.”
  2. Ask one van group: “How many cards are In Progress?” Max four. Then: “Is the van blocked, or are you pretending it is moving?”
  3. Ask one Friendsgiving group: “Where is the fountain?” It should sit in Backlog. Then: “Does Jordan still have a Sunday card?”
  4. Close: a board is how you talk when someone missed the chat. Four jobs, four names. Extra wishes wait.

Early finisher

Add one label color for Blocked vs Due today. Or park one more wish on the later list with a comment. Same board. Do not start a second product.
