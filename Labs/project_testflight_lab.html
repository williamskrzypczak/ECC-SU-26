TestFlight Basics

Introduction

This lab walks you through distributing an iOS app with TestFlight. You will create an App Store Connect record, archive a build in Xcode, upload it, and install it on a physical device.

TestFlight is a private dress rehearsal. The App Store is opening night. Testers install your build before Apple reviews it for the public store.

Paid Apple Developer Program required. A free Apple ID can use the simulator. TestFlight needs an active paid membership. Enroll at developer.apple.com/programs (opens in a new tab) before you start.

Learning Objectives

After this lab, you will be able to:

  • Create an app record in App Store Connect
  • Archive an iOS build in Xcode and upload it
  • Prepare a build for TestFlight testing
  • Install your build on a physical device with the TestFlight app
  • Invite your instructor as a TestFlight beta tester

Prerequisites

  • A Mac with Xcode 15 or later
  • An active paid Apple Developer Program membership
  • A physical iPhone or iPad signed in with your Apple ID
  • Your semester project app, or another complete iOS app that runs without crash on a device

If your project is not archive-ready yet, use a small working app with a clear bundle ID. Do not submit a broken build.

Lab Instructions

Part 1: Confirm membership and open App Store Connect

  1. Sign in at developer.apple.com/account (opens in a new tab).
  2. Confirm your membership status shows as active.
  3. Open appstoreconnect.apple.com (opens in a new tab) and sign in with the same Apple ID.
  4. In Xcode, go to Xcode → Settings → Accounts. Add this Apple ID if it is missing.

Part 2: Create or open the app record

  1. In App Store Connect, open My Apps.
  2. Click the plus button and choose New App, or open an existing app that matches your project.
  3. Choose iOS. Enter an app name, primary language, and bundle ID that matches your Xcode target.
  4. Save the record. Take a screenshot of the Apps list or the app information page showing your app name and bundle ID. Save it as app-store-connect-app-record.png.

Part 3: Archive and upload from Xcode

  1. Open your project in Xcode. Select your app target.
  2. Under Signing & Capabilities, turn on Automatically manage signing. Choose your paid team.
  3. Set the scheme destination to Any iOS Device (arm64). You cannot archive for a simulator.
  4. Choose Product → Archive. Wait for the Organizer window.
  5. Select the new archive. Click Distribute App.
  6. Choose App Store Connect, then upload. Keep the default options unless your instructor says otherwise.
  7. Take a screenshot of the Organizer showing your archive. Save it as xcode-organizer-archive.png.

Part 4: Prepare the build in TestFlight

  1. Return to App Store Connect. Open your app.
  2. Open the TestFlight tab. Wait until the build status is Processing or Valid.
  3. Complete any Export Compliance questions if Apple asks. Most classroom apps that use only standard HTTPS answer No for non-exempt encryption.
  4. Enter short What to Test notes and a feedback email.
  5. Take a screenshot of TestFlight or Activity showing this build and its status. Save it as testflight-build.png.

Part 5: Install on a device and invite your instructor

  1. On your iPhone or iPad, install the free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  2. In App Store Connect, add yourself as an internal tester for this build if you are not already listed.
  3. Accept the invite on the device. Install your app from TestFlight.
  4. Open the app once to confirm it launches.
  5. Take a screenshot of the TestFlight app showing your build, or of your app running after a TestFlight install. Save it as testflight-on-device.png.
  6. In App Store Connect → TestFlight, create or open an External Testing group (or Internal Testing if your account allows adding others).
  7. Add bill.skrzypczak@gmail.com as an invited beta tester and send the invite. Do not skip this step.
  8. Take a screenshot of the tester list that clearly shows bill.skrzypczak@gmail.com. Save it as testflight-instructor-invite.png.

Reflection Questions

Answer each question in one or two complete sentences in reflections.txt.

  1. What is the difference between Run on a simulator and Archive for TestFlight?
  2. What is the difference between an internal tester and an external tester?
  3. Why use TestFlight before you submit the app for App Store review?
  4. What do the build statuses Processing and Valid mean?

Troubleshooting

Archive is grayed out

Select Any iOS Device (arm64) as the run destination. Simulator destinations cannot create an App Store archive.

Signing errors

Confirm your paid team is selected under Signing & Capabilities. The bundle ID must match the App Store Connect record.

Build stuck on Processing

Wait 10 to 30 minutes. Check the email on your Apple ID for validation errors. Refresh the TestFlight tab.

Missing Export Compliance

Open the build in TestFlight and answer the encryption questions. Until you finish them, the build cannot go to testers.

Invite never arrives

Confirm the device Apple ID matches the tester email. Open the TestFlight app and pull to refresh. Check spam for the App Store Connect message.

How to Submit on Canvas

Package your work as one zip file. Use the exact folder structure, screenshot names, and reflection format in the TestFlight submission guide.

Create your zip on a Mac

  1. Create a folder on your Desktop with every required file from the submission guide inside.
  2. Right-click the folder in Finder and choose Compress "folder name".
  3. Rename the zip file if needed (example: testflight-smith-jane.zip).
  4. Upload the zip to this Canvas assignment.
