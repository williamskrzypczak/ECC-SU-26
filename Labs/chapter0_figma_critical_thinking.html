Chapter 0 In-Class: Figma Decisions

You already sketched a welcome screen in Figma. This is not more drawing. It is “what would you do next, and why?”

Setup

Work in groups of three for about 15 minutes. Pick one recorder. That person writes the group’s answers.

Keep your Campus Welcome Wireframe file nearby if you want. Do not start a new Figma file. Do not open Xcode.

Key Ideas

Figma is your sketchbook. A floor plan comes before you build the house. A frame is one phone screen.

Auto layout stacks items like a SwiftUI VStack. A component is a stamp. An instance is a copy still linked to that stamp.

A prototype is the tap that opens the next screen. Export saves the outer frame as a PNG, not a loose text layer.

How to Play

  1. Read each card out loud.
  2. Agree on one next move and one sentence why.
  3. Write what goes wrong if you pick the wrong move.
  4. Finish all six cards in about six minutes. Then we compare answers.

Pick one next move per card:

  • Create an iPhone frame and put the layers inside
  • Apply Auto layout
  • Create a component, then use instances
  • Duplicate the frame
  • Connect a prototype, then Present
  • Select the outer frame, then export

The Cards

Card A — Drawing with no phone

You typed a title and drew a button on the empty canvas. There is no iPhone outline. The layers sit in open space.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you keep drawing on the blank canvas?

Card B — The stack falls apart

You lined up the title, name, tagline, and button by hand. You move the title. The rest stay put. The gaps look uneven.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you keep nudging each piece?

Card C — The copy does not update

You copy-pasted Get Started onto the Detail Screen. You change the first button to blue. The copy on Detail Screen stays gray.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you keep pasting more copies?

Card D — You need a second screen

Welcome Screen looks good. You want a Detail Screen to tap into. Starting from a blank canvas feels slow and easy to mismatch.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you draw a tiny new rectangle instead?

Card E — The click does nothing

You have two frames. You click Get Started on the Design canvas. Nothing happens. There is no blue arrow.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you keep clicking in Design view?

Card F — The PNG looks wrong

You hit Export. The PNG is a tiny piece of text, or it looks empty. The layers panel shows a text layer selected, not Welcome Screen.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you export that inner layer?

Exit Tickets

As a group, finish these two sentences. Write them on the recorder’s notes.

A frame is a phone screen. A group is ________________________________.

I use a component so that when I change the button, ________________________________.
