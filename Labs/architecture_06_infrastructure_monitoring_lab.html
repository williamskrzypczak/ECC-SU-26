Architecture Lab 6: Infrastructure and Monitoring

This is step 6 of 7. Plan on about 40 minutes with your team.

Introduction

A change can ship and still go quiet overnight. This lab is about the ground under your app.

You will draw a small health board in the same Figma file. You do not need a brand name.

Work with your team for about 40 minutes. Then each person turns in their own zip.

Use the same Figma link as the earlier labs. Your reflection must be your own words.

Learning Objectives

After this lab, you will be able to:

  • Say where saved information lives when the phone is in a pocket
  • Name three signs the app is healthy
  • Separate one alarm from one problem that can wait
  • Say what users lose if the ground is down for an hour

Key Ideas

Infrastructure is the building under the shop. Monitoring is the smoke alarm. The phone is only the front door.

When the phone is in a pocket, the saved information still has to live somewhere. You need a way to know the building is healthy in the morning.

Prerequisites

Finish Figma Basics. Your team needs a project you can describe in one sentence.

You need a free Figma account. Create one at figma.com/signup if you do not have one yet.

Lab Instructions

Part 1: Talk through the questions

Read these out loud. Answer them for your app, not for a sample app. Give this about 8 minutes.

  1. When the phone is in a pocket, where does the saved information live?
  2. What are three signs the app is healthy in the morning?
  3. What is one problem worth an alarm, and one that can wait?
  4. What do users lose if that ground is down for an hour?

Part 2: Build the frame

Give this about 25 minutes. A classmate should understand the frame in 60 seconds.

  1. Open the same team file. Do not start a new file.
  2. Add a frame. Name it 6 Infrastructure and Monitoring.
  3. Draw three lights. Label them Up, Slow, and Errors.
  4. Next to each light, write what it means for your app.
  5. Add one sentence: where the app runs when the phone is in a pocket. “A computer in the cloud” is enough.
  6. Add two short notes. One is the alarm. One is the problem that can wait.

Your frame is done when it has all of these:

  • Three lights: Up, Slow, and Errors
  • A plain meaning for each light in your app
  • One sentence about where the app runs
  • One alarm worth acting on, and one problem that can wait

Part 3: Share and export

  1. Click Share in the top right.
  2. Set access to Anyone with the link can view.
  3. Copy the link. You will paste it in links.txt.
  4. Select the frame named 6 Infrastructure and Monitoring.
  5. In the properties panel, scroll to Export. Click +. Choose PNG at 1x.
  6. Export the frame. Save it as architecture-6-infrastructure.png.

Put that PNG in a folder named screenshots. The file name must match exactly.

Reflection Questions

Answer in your own words. Write at least two sentences for each question. Do not copy a teammate.

  1. Where does your app’s saved information live when the phone is in a pocket?
  2. What problem is worth an alarm, and what can wait?
  3. What do users lose if the app is down for an hour?

Deliverables

Submit one zip file named architecture6-infrastructure-lastname-firstname.zip. The team may share one Figma link. Each student still writes their own reflection.

  • links.txt with one line: Figma: and your view link
  • reflections.txt with Q1:, Q2:, and Q3:
  • screenshots/architecture-6-infrastructure.png

Troubleshooting

Share link asks people to log in

Click Share. Set access to Anyone with the link can view. Copy that link into links.txt.

The export is blank or cropped

Select the outer frame named 6 Infrastructure and Monitoring. Do not select a text box inside it.

The board only says “server”

Say what Up, Slow, and Errors mean for your app. A word with no meaning is not a check.

You named a product you have not used

You do not need a brand. Say where the information lives in plain words.

How to Submit on Canvas

Package your work as one zip file. Use the exact names in the Architecture Lab 6 submission guide.

Create your zip on a Mac

  1. Create a folder named architecture6-infrastructure-lastname-firstname.
  2. Put links.txt, reflections.txt, and the screenshots folder inside it.
  3. Right-click the folder and choose Compress.
  4. Rename the zip if needed, then upload it to this Canvas assignment.

Compress the folder, not the loose files. See the Mac zip steps if you get stuck.
