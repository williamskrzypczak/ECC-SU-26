Working Backwards 3: Interface and Interaction

This is step 3 of 5. Plan on about 15 minutes with your team. There is no Canvas upload.

You know the moment and the benefit. This step is how they touch the product.

Key idea

Start with the customer’s moment. Technology comes last.

The first 10 seconds are the front door. If they have to figure out the door, they never come in.

Questions

Answer these for your app. Read them out loud.

  1. How do they start? Do they tap, type, or talk?
  2. What should the first 10 seconds feel like?
  3. What should they not have to figure out?
  4. What is the one thing they touch first?

You can say this in one minute

You can describe the first touch out loud. A paper sketch is enough. Figma is optional, and you do not turn it in.
