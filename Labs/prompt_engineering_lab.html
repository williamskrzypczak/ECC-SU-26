In-Class Lab: Prompt Engineering

This is a team contest. It takes about 30 minutes. There is no Canvas upload.

The poster

Your job is to make a picture as close to this poster as you can. The words you type are the only thing the tool knows.

Key idea

A prompt is a takeout order. “Something good” gets you a surprise. The cook only knows the words you wrote.

A closer picture usually comes from a clearer order, not from a longer one.

How to play

Work in teams of about four. Several teams play at once. The team whose picture is closest to the poster wins.

  1. Look at the poster. Do not upload it into the AI. Recreating from a picture skips the point.
  2. Pick any image tool your team already has.
  3. Write one prompt together. Run it.
  4. You get five tries. Change one thing at a time. Keep the prompt that got closest.
  5. Put the final picture and the final prompt on one slide, or hold them up.
  6. The class looks. Closest to the poster wins. The instructor breaks a tie.

What to put in the prompt

Say these four things in your own words. The poster is the spec. This page will not describe it for you.

  • What kind of picture it is. A vertical movie poster, illustrated, not a photo.
  • The exact words that must appear, spelled the way you see them.
  • What sits in front, in the middle, and in the back.
  • What to leave out.

After the vote

Take about five minutes. Answer these out loud. There is no single right answer.

  1. Which words did the tool get right, and which did it invent?
  2. What happened to the tiny credit block at the bottom?
  3. Did a longer prompt help, or did it muddy the picture?
