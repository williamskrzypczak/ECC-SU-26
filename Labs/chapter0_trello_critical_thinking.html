Chapter 0 In-Class: Trello Decisions

You already planned Campus Welcome tasks in Trello. This is not more clicking. It is “what would you do next, and why?”

Setup

Work in groups of three for about 15 minutes. Pick one recorder. That person writes the group’s answers.

Keep your Campus Welcome App Sprint board nearby if you want. Do not create a new board. Do not open Xcode.

Key Ideas

Trello is sticky notes on a whiteboard. A board is one project. A list is a column, like To Do or Done.

A card is one sticky note. A label is a color category. A checklist is the small steps on that one note.

Drag a card right to show progress. A comment is a note on the task. Share the board so a classmate can open it.

How to Play

  1. Read each card out loud.
  2. Agree on one next move and one sentence why.
  3. Write what goes wrong if you pick the wrong move.
  4. Finish all six cards in about six minutes. Then we compare answers.

Pick one next move per card:

  • Create To Do, In Progress, and Done lists
  • Add a card (not a new list)
  • Add a checklist
  • Apply labels and show them on the front
  • Move the card to Done, then comment
  • Share the board link

The Cards

Card A — Everything in one pile

You have a board. All four tasks sit in one unnamed column. You cannot tell what is started and what is finished.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you keep one pile?

Card B — The column is named like a task

You renamed a list to Set up Xcode project because that is one task. The column is now a single job, not a stage.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if lists are named after tasks?

Card C — One huge card

The Build ContentView welcome screen card has title text, a student name, modifiers, and a preview. You keep forgetting a piece.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you dump it all in the description with no checkboxes?

Card D — You cannot see priority

Four cards look the same on the board. You cannot tell Setup from Coding or High priority unless you open each card.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if colors stay hidden?

Card E — The work is done, the card is not

You created the Xcode project. The card still sits in To Do. Teammates think you have not started.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you delete the card instead?

Card F — The classmate cannot get in

You pasted the board URL in chat. They hit a login wall, or the board looks private.

What do you do next? What goes wrong if you only send a screenshot?

Exit Tickets

As a group, finish these two sentences. Write them on the recorder’s notes.

A list is a column or stage. A card is ________________________________.

I move a card to Done so that ________________________________.
