Working Backwards 2: Customer Benefits

This is step 2 of 5. Plan on about 15 minutes with your team. There is no Canvas upload.

You already named the moment. This step asks why that moment matters to them.

Key idea

Start with the customer’s moment. Technology comes last.

A benefit is the reason they would cross the street for it. A nice extra is not a reason.

Questions

Answer these for your app. Read them out loud.

  1. What pain goes away?
  2. What benefit is strong enough that they would tell a friend?
  3. What benefit sounds nice but is not the point?
  4. What would the customer say, in their own words?

You can say this in one minute

You can name one pain and one benefit in the customer’s words.
