Chapter 0 In-Class: Trello Agile Challenges

You already made a Trello board for a fake sprint. This is not more clicking through menus. Your group plans one messy college weekend on a real board.

Setup

Work in groups of four for about 20 to 25 minutes. Pick one recorder. That person names the board and keeps it shared.

  • Pick one of the three challenges. Do not do all three.
  • Start a new Trello board. Do not reuse Campus Welcome App Sprint.
  • Every must-do card gets one owner. A card with no name will not happen.
  • Talk on the cards. Group chat does not count as an update.

Key Ideas

A board is the group chat you can still read tomorrow. Lists are buckets. Cards are jobs. One name on a card means that person owns it.

Tonight’s work is small. New wishes go to a later list. Finish one card before you start three more.

How to Play

  1. Read all three challenges out loud. Vote. Pick one.
  2. Assign the four named people in that challenge to the four of you.
  3. Argue for about five minutes. What would a clumsy board look like? What would a clear board look like?
  4. Build the board in Trello. Name the board. Put one name on each must-do card.
  5. Be ready to show the board for about 60 seconds.

Challenge 1: RA Inspection Friday

Email at 9 p.m. Room inspection is Friday at 10 a.m. Four zones will get checked: kitchen, bathroom, living room, dumpster. Fail one zone, lose $200. The group chat is “I’ll do it later.”

The four people

  • Jules has the only car. Trash bags already sit in the hall. Jules keeps saying, “I’ll swing by later.”
  • Kai lives in the kitchen mess. Nobody else knows which Tupperware is science.
  • Morgan has the only key to the hall closet. The vacuum is locked in there.
  • Riley has an exam Thursday night and the only plunger. Riley will not be awake before 8 a.m.

Rules

  • Four Friday cards. Four different owners. A zone with no name fails.
  • Inspection at 10 a.m. is the finish line. Hallway yelling is not an update.
  • One person, one zone. Do not give anyone a second zone.
  • Nice-to-clean stuff is allowed. It does not live on the Friday list.

Your board is done when

Each zone has a named card and a Friday due date. One card is Done with a comment from that person, such as “bags at the chute, 9:40.” You can tell who owns dumpster, kitchen, vacuum, and bathroom without asking.

Trello: Board RA Friday. Exactly four must-do cards, four owners, Friday due dates. One Done comment.

Challenge 2: Spring Break in One Van

Four friends. One van. Leave Friday 6 a.m. Nobody booked the Airbnb. Three people already bought snacks. One person is still “looking at beaches.” Two people think they are driving first. The group chat has 80 messages and zero decisions.

The four people

  • Casey is the vibe captain. Picks the playlist. Books nothing. Still “looking at beaches.”
  • Priya is the money-worrier. Three people already bought snacks. Priya is already anxious about Venmo.
  • Devon is the overpacker. Started packing three suitcases. Has not thought about what actually fits in the van.
  • Alex is the driver who might flake. Hasn’t confirmed the car. Two people think they are driving first.

Rules

  • Max one In Progress card per person. Four people means four In Progress cards, not twelve.
  • Split “plan the trip” into real tasks. Playlist is not the trip.
  • If you are waiting on Alex, that card is blocked. Do not fake that it is moving.
  • Friday 6 a.m. is the finish line.

Your board is done when

The board shows who books the room, who confirms the van, who collects gas money, and who handles the cooler. At most four cards sit in In Progress. Gas money uses a checklist. Confirm van has a blocked note if Alex has not said yes.

Trello: Board Van Friday 6am. At most four In Progress cards, each with a member. One split card with a checklist. One blocked note.

Challenge 3: Friendsgiving Scope Creep

Four roommates said “cheap dinner, four people, Sunday.” By Wednesday the Instagram story says “OPEN HOUSE.” One oven. Budget $60. At 1 a.m. Sam DMs: “quick add: chocolate fountain and a seating chart.”

The four people

  • Avery is the only one who has used this oven. Shiny ideas pull Avery away from the knob.
  • Sam has the car and already bought groceries. Sam is also the 1 a.m. fairy.
  • Jordan is 21. The group chat keeps asking Jordan to grab something on the way.
  • Riley keeps the extra chairs in a closet. The table is still covered in textbooks.

Rules

  • Four This-Sunday cards. Four different owners.
  • The fountain is extra. It does not steal the oven or the grocery run.
  • New ideas go to a later list. Do not start them in a DM.
  • Write the “not this Sunday” note on the card, where everyone can see it.

Your board is done when

Turkey, sides, drinks, and clean each have a name and a Sunday due date. The fountain and the seating chart sit on a later list with no Sunday due date. One polite comment parks the extra. The grocery run is still a real Sunday job.

Trello: Board Friendsgiving. Four This-Sunday cards with four different members. At least two later-list cards that used to be DMs. One polite park comment.

Exit Tickets

As a group, finish these two sentences. Write them on the recorder’s notes.

1. We put one name on each card so that ________________________________.

2. A new wish that is not tonight’s work should go ________________________________.
