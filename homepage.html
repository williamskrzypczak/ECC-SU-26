Course Policies

Click the MCC Syllabus link on the left to view the syllabus.

Mac and Xcode: You need a Mac with Xcode to complete the SwiftUI labs in this course. If you do not have one, contact me. I will connect you with IT about a loaner device.

Laptop or desktop: You also need a computer for online tool labs such as GitHub, Figma, Trello, Slack, and Firebase.

Late work: Late work loses 5% of maximum points per day. It will not be accepted after one week or the last day of class or the final exam date. Being absent from class does not change your due date.

Attendance: Although this is an online course, you are expected to check in weekly. Assignments are due one week after they are assigned. Weekly participation is essential for success. Completing the material is your responsibility. Contact your instructor during a personal emergency and be prepared to document reasons for late work.
